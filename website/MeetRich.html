His story他的故事

From newcomer to neighbour從新移民到社區的一份子

Born in Hong Kong, Ritch Lau immigrated to Canada with his family in 2000 at the age of 14. Like many newcomers, he experienced first-hand the challenges of adapting to a new country, learning a new culture, and building a life in Canada. These experiences shaped his appreciation for community, opportunity, and the importance of helping residents feel heard and supported. 劉肇麟出生於香港，2000年在14歲時隨家人移民加拿大。和許多新移民一樣，他親身經歷了適應新國家、學習新文化以及在加拿大建立新生活的挑戰。這些經歷讓他深深體會社區的重要性，以及讓居民感到被聆聽及支持的意義。

Ritch lives in Markham with his wife, Loretta, their daughter, Leia, and their younger son, Leon. As a husband, father, and local Ward 2 resident, he understands that municipal issues are often deeply personal — whether they involve neighbourhood safety, traffic, parks, trees, local services, or quality of life concerns. 劉肇麟與太太Loretta、女兒Leia及兒子Leon一同居於萬錦市。身為丈夫、父親及第二選區居民，他明白市政議題往往與生活息息相關 — 無論是社區安全、交通、公園、樹木、地區服務，還是生活質素的種種考量。

Before entering public service, Ritch spent more than 15 years in media and journalism as a radio host, reporter, and television news anchor. That experience shaped his belief in listening carefully, communicating clearly, and giving people a voice. 在投身公共服務之前，劉肇麟從事傳媒及新聞行業超過15年，曾擔任電台主持、記者及電視新聞主播。這段經歷塑造了他細心聆聽、清晰溝通及為民發聲的信念。

Ritch holds an Honours Specialist Degree in Mental Health Studies from the University of Toronto Scarborough and a Diploma in Early Childhood Education. His educational background has strengthened his understanding of families, children, mental wellness, and the importance of building inclusive and caring communities. 劉肇麟持有多倫多大學士嘉堡分校精神健康研究榮譽學位，以及幼兒教育文憑。他的學術背景加深了他對家庭、兒童、精神健康，以及建立共融友愛社區重要性的理解。

Elected as Ward 2 Councillor in the 2022 Municipal Election, Ritch serves the communities of Civic Centre, Buttonville, Cachet, Cathedraltown, Victoria Square, and Springwater. He is committed to being accessible and responsive to residents across these neighbourhoods. A major part of Ritch's daily work is helping residents navigate City services and making sure their concerns are heard, whether through emails, phone calls, or in-person conversations. 劉肇麟於2022年市政選舉當選第二選區市議員，服務Civic Centre、Buttonville、Cachet、Cathedraltown、Victoria Square及Springwater各社區。他致力於保持與居民的緊密聯繫及回應。他日常工作的重要部分，是協助居民處理市府服務事宜，並確保他們的聲音獲得聆聽 — 無論是透過電郵、電話，還是親身對話。
